Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information tonic.fm ("we," "us") collects, why, and how you can control it. It applies to anyone who uses tonic.fm (the "Service").

This is a template, not legal advice — have a lawyer confirm this fully complies with the laws that apply to your actual users (e.g., GDPR if you have EU/UK users, CCPA if you have California users) before publishing it.

1. Information We Collect

Account information you provide: - Email address and password (stored securely/hashed — see Section 4) - Username, bio, and profile photo (optional) - Password reset requests

Content you create: - Song entries, tiers, notes, "reminds me of" tags, stacks, and other catalogue data - Photos and stickers you upload - Messages you send to friends within the Service - Friend connections and requests

Automatically collected information: - Basic usage/analytics data (e.g., pages visited, feature usage) if analytics tools are enabled - Device/browser information and IP address, typically collected automatically by hosting infrastructure - Local browser storage for preferences like theme customization (this stays on your device)

We do not currently collect: payment information (the Service has no payments at this time).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Let you catalogue music, connect with friends, and message other users
  • Show your public/shared content (cataloguex cards, tier boards) to the audience you choose
  • Send account-related emails (password resets, notifications you opt into)
  • Maintain and improve the Service, including basic usage analytics
  • Detect and prevent abuse, fraud, or violations of our Terms of Service

We do not sell your personal information.

3. How Information Is Shared

  • With other users: Depending on your settings, your profile, catalogue, tier board, or shareable card may be visible to friends or, if you generate a public share link/image, to anyone with that link.
  • Messages are visible to you and the friend you're messaging; we don't use message content for advertising.
  • Service providers: We may use third parties for hosting, email delivery, or analytics, who process data on our behalf under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal reasons: We may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of tonic.fm or others.
  • We do not share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data (e.g., password hashing, restricted access), but no system is 100% secure. Please use a unique, strong password and let us know at samannleblanc@gmail.com if you notice suspicious activity.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where we're required to retain it for legal reasons.

6. Your Choices and Rights

  • Access/edit: You can view and edit most of your data directly in account/profile settings.
  • Export: The Service includes an export feature so you can download a backup of your catalogue.
  • Delete: You can delete individual entries, or request full account deletion by contacting samannleblanc@gmail.com.
  • Notifications/emails: You can opt out of non-essential emails via settings or the unsubscribe link.

If you're in the EU/UK/EEA (GDPR): You have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port your data, and to object to certain processing. Our legal bases for processing are performance of a contract (running your account), legitimate interests (security, improving the Service), and consent (optional communications). You can lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you're in California (CCPA/CPRA): You have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

7. Children's Privacy

tonic.fm is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us at samannleblanc@gmail.com and we'll remove it.

8. International Users

Because the Service is available globally, your data may be processed in a country other than your own. By using the Service, you consent to this transfer, subject to the protections described in this policy.

9. Cookies and Local Storage

We may use cookies or local browser storage for essential functions (staying logged in) and, if enabled, basic analytics. (Add a cookie banner/consent tool if you have EU users and use non-essential cookies, since GDPR/ePrivacy generally requires opt-in consent for those.)
